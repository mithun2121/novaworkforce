About NOVA

Built around the work businesses already do.

NOVA Workforce is an early-stage product project exploring structured, reviewable workflows for small businesses and service teams.

What we are building

The current preview provides workflow templates, a mission brief builder, supported text file handling and an AI mission endpoint that can be configured in Cloudflare. It does not connect business data sources or external actions by default.

What we value

Specific capabilities, clear limits, and human review. We aim to publish only capabilities and results that can be substantiated.

Current stage

NOVA is in development. Pricing, integrations and product capabilities will be confirmed as they are validated.
