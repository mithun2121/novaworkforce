Product boundaries

Security and responsible use.

The current preview is early-stage. Sensitive capabilities must be configured before they are used for real business operations.

Do not submit secrets

Do not upload passwords, API keys, payment card details, personal identification documents or confidential customer records into the public workflow preview. Use only information you are authorised to share.

AI output requires review

Generated work may be incomplete or incorrect. Model-reported checks are not independent proof of correctness. Review material claims and evidence before relying on them.

Connected actions

Business APIs, email sending, publishing, purchases and record updates are not automatically connected by default. Configure the relevant permissions and approvals before any external action is considered.

Security questions

Please report security concerns to hello@novaworkforce.online. Avoid including live secrets in the report.
