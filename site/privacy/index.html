Privacy Policy

How NOVA Workforce handles information provided through this public website.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Who this notice covers

This notice describes how the NOVA Workforce project and brand ("NOVA", "we", "us") handle information when you visit novaworkforce.online, contact us, or interact with the current public website. NOVA is an early-stage product project. This notice is a practical description of the current site, not a representation that all planned product systems are live.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by email or use the early-access form, you may provide your name, work email, company, role and a description of a workflow. The current early-access form opens a pre-filled email draft. The website does not submit that draft automatically; you choose whether to send it from your email app.

3. Technical information

The hosting and network providers used to deliver the website may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and security events as part of normal hosting and abuse prevention. We do not intentionally add advertising trackers or analytics pixels to this version of the website.

4. How information is used

Information you send may be used to respond to your enquiry, evaluate early-access interest, understand requested workflows, maintain business correspondence, protect the website and comply with applicable law.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Website hosting, domain, DNS, email and infrastructure providers may process information to provide their services. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably needed to protect rights, safety or the security of NOVA or others. Providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Retention and security

We aim to keep enquiries only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose received, ordinary business records, security, dispute resolution or legal obligations. No website can guarantee absolute security. Please do not include passwords, payment card details, special-category personal information or confidential customer records in an early-access enquiry.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not intentionally set non-essential analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may use necessary security technologies depending on the configuration. See the Cookie Notice for more information.

8. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to us, or object to certain processing. Contact hello@novaworkforce.online. We may need to verify the request before responding.

9. Children

This website is intended for business and general audiences and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through the public site.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or product changes. Updates will be posted here with a revised date. For privacy questions, email hello@novaworkforce.online.
